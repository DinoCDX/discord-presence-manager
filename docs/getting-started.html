Getting set up

It takes a few minutes, and most of it is tapping through permission screens.

  1. Download the APK from the latest release and open it. The first time, Android asks whether your browser or file manager may install apps. Allow it.
  2. Open DPM. The first screens are the intro, then Log in with Discord. Tap Continue with Discord. You approve DPM on Discord's own login page, so captchas, two-factor codes and passkeys all work.
  3. Go through the permissions page and allow what you're comfortable with. Every permission can be skipped and changed later. The permissions page explains each one.
  4. On the Presence tab, switch on Share my presence.
  5. On the Apps tab, choose which apps are allowed to show up.

Finding your way around

Presence

The on/off switch and pause button, a live preview, dynamic or static mode, your schedule and the online-only option.

Apps

Every app on your phone, with search. Choose whether each one shows, is hidden, or gets its own text.

Settings

Accounts, appearance, presence options, pictures, updates and About. Tap the version on About seven times to unlock developer options.

While DPM is running you'll see a notification with Pause and Stop buttons, so you can control it without opening the app.
