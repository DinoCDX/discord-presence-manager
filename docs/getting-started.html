Getting set up

It takes a few minutes, and most of it is tapping through permission screens.

  1. Grab the APK from the latest release and open it. The first time, Android will ask if your browser or file manager is allowed to install apps. Say yes.
  2. Log in. The Browser option is the easiest because it uses Discord's own login page, so captchas, two-factor codes and passkeys all work. Token and QR are there if you'd rather use those.
  3. Click through the intro and allow what you're comfortable with. You can skip any of it and change your mind later. There's a rundown of each permission.
  4. On the Presence tab, switch on Share my presence.
  5. Over on the Apps tab, pick which apps are allowed to show up.
  6. If you want app icons and cover art, follow the images guide. It's optional.

Finding your way around

TabWhat's in it
PresenceThe on/off switch and pause button, a live preview, dynamic or static mode, your schedule and the online-only option.
AppsEvery app on your phone, with search. Allow or ignore each one, or give it custom text.
SettingsAccounts, theme, images, background options, updates and your token.

While DPM is running you'll see a notification with Pause and Stop buttons, so you can control it without opening the app.
