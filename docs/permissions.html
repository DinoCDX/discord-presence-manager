Permissions

DPM asks for quite a few things. Here's what each one is for, and what happens if you say no.

PermissionWhat it's forIf you skip it
InternetTalking to Discord and GitHub.Nothing works without it.
NotificationsThe notification with Pause and Stop.Sharing still works, you just lose the quick buttons.
Usage accessSeeing which app or game is on screen.Only music and video will show.
Notification accessReading the title, artist and cover of what's playing.DPM can't tell when media is playing.
Battery optimizationStopping Android from putting DPM to sleep.Presence may drop out when the phone sits idle.
Start at bootBringing presence back after a restart.You'll need to open the app yourself after rebooting.
Installed appsBuilding the Apps tab and showing icons.You lose per-app control.
Install packagesInstalling updates you've downloaded.You can still update by installing the APK by hand.

Your token stays on the phone in encrypted storage, and the only place it's ever sent is Discord.

DPM (Discord Presence Manager) isn't affiliated with Discord. Source on GitHub
