Troubleshooting

Pick the problem you're having. Most fixes take less than a minute.

Nothing shows up on Discord

First check that Share my presence is on and not paused. The line under the switch tells you what's holding it back, like a schedule or the online-only option. In dynamic mode, the app also needs to be allowed on the Apps tab, and Usage access needs to be granted.

My music or video isn't picked up

DPM reads what's playing through Notification access, so make sure that's on. Also check the player isn't set to Never on the Apps tab.

Presence stops after a while

Go to Settings, open the Presence card, and tap Allow background activity. That button only appears while DPM isn't exempt, so if you can't see it, you're already set. Phones from Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei and Samsung often hide a second autostart or app launch setting in system settings. That one needs to be on too.

It says Reconnecting for ages

While Discord or your connection is down, DPM keeps retrying with longer and longer pauses, up to a minute. It'll reconnect by itself as soon as things are back.

Pictures are missing

Turn on Send pictures under Settings, Pictures. Album art and static pictures need a direct image link ending in .png, .jpg, .gif, .webp or .avif. Your own Discord app is optional, under Settings, Developer. The Pictures section explains each option.

I can't log in

Log in with Discord. It signs in on Discord's own page, so captchas, two-factor codes and passkeys behave normally. DPM renews your login in the background. If it says Discord signed you out, log in again.

An update won't install

Android needs your permission to let DPM install apps. Tap Install again and allow it when the settings page opens. If Android says the app wasn't installed, that release was probably signed with a different key from the one on your phone. Uninstall DPM and install the new APK.

The themed icon isn't showing

The themed icon needs Android 13, plus themed icons turned on in your launcher.
