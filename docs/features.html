Features

Dynamic mode

DPM watches what you're doing and picks something to show. Music and video, games and other apps can each be turned on or off and put in priority order; the top one with something to show wins. Games always show up, and other apps show up once you've allowed them on the Apps tab. Optional buttons link to YouTube and to the DPM page.

Static mode

Prefer to write it yourself? Pick a type (Playing, Listening to, Watching or Competing in), then fill in a name, details and state. You can add large and small images, a timer (elapsed or countdown), up to two buttons, and save presets. The preview updates as you type, and everything saves on its own.

The preview

The card on the Presence tab is meant to look like the real thing on Discord: cover art, a little app icon in the corner and a progress bar for music and video.

Apps

  • One switch allows the popular ones, like YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • Another allows everything else. Anything you've ignored stays hidden.
  • Each app can be Auto, Allowed or Ignored. Ignored apps never show, even if they're playing music.
  • Any app can also get its own type, name, details and state.

Schedule and rules

  • A time window is a set of days plus a start and end time. If it ends before it starts, it carries on into the next day.
  • "Only when online" keeps your presence hidden while your status is Idle, Do Not Disturb or Invisible.
  • Pause stops sharing for now without turning DPM off.

Accounts

  • Add accounts through the browser, a token or a QR code.
  • Switching is instant, and presence moves over to the new account.
  • You can show or copy the active account's token. Removing an account only removes it from this phone.

Running in the background

DPM runs as a foreground service, which is why there's a notification. It can start again after a reboot, it checks less often while your screen is off, and it backs off politely when Discord can't be reached.

Updates

When you open the app, DPM asks GitHub whether there's a newer release, at most once every 12 hours. If there is, you can download it right there. Before the install prompt appears, the file is checked against its published size and SHA-256 hash. You can turn the automatic check off in Settings, or check by hand whenever you want.
