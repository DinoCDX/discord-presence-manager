Discord presence for your phone

DPM shows the music, video, games and apps you use on Android as your Discord activity.

Download Setup guide

Sending presence from a user account is against Discord's Terms of Service and can get the account limited or banned. Use an alternate account if you can.

What it does

Follows what you do

Playing media comes first, then the game or app on screen.

Custom text

Write your own activity, or change the text for any single app.

Schedule

Share only on certain days and hours.

Several accounts

Log in with the browser, a token or a QR code and switch between them.

Updates

Checks GitHub for new releases and installs them from inside the app.

Material You

Follows your wallpaper colors, light and dark mode, themed icon.

Needs Android 10 or newer. Wallpaper colors need Android 12, the themed icon needs Android 13.
