Features and benefits

Follows along

Whatever's playing comes first. If nothing is, it shows the game or app you're in.

Says it your way

Write your own status, or give any single app its own text.

Keeps office hours

Choose the days and times it's allowed to show, or only share while you're online.

Material 3 Expressive

Bouncy, rounded and in your wallpaper colors, with a themed icon too.

Get started

  1. Download the APK

    Get it from the latest release and open it.

  2. Log in with Discord

    You approve DPM on Discord's own login page.

  3. Allow the permissions you're comfortable with

    Every permission can be skipped and changed later.

  4. Switch on Share my presence

    Then choose which apps can show up on the Apps tab.
