Discord Presence Manager

Show what you're up to on Discord, from your phone

DPM turns the music you're playing, the videos you're watching and the games and apps you have open into your Discord activity.

Download the APK Get set up
Android 10+Open source

What it does

Follows along

Whatever's playing comes first. If nothing is, it shows the game or app you're in.

Says it your way

Write your own status, or give any single app its own text.

Keeps office hours

Choose the days and times it's allowed to show, or only share while you're online.

More than one account

Sign in with Discord and switch between them whenever you like.

Updates in the app

It looks for new releases on GitHub and lets you install them without leaving DPM.

Material 3 Expressive

Bouncy, rounded and in your wallpaper colors, with a themed icon too.

Where to start

Setup

Install, log in and start sharing in about five minutes.

Features

Dynamic and static modes, per-app control, schedules and accounts.

Troubleshooting

Fixes for presence that won't show, drops out or won't update.

You'll need Android 10 or newer. Wallpaper colors need Android 12, and the themed icon needs Android 13.
